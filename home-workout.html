Effective Home Workout Routines

Build muscle and get fit at home with minimal or no equipment.

Essential Bodyweight Exercises

  • Push-ups: 3-4 sets of 10-20 reps - Chest, shoulders, triceps
  • Pull-ups: 3-4 sets of 5-10 reps - Back and biceps (use a door frame bar)
  • Squats: 4 sets of 15-25 reps - Legs and glutes
  • Planks: 3 sets of 30-60 seconds - Core strength
  • Lunges: 3 sets of 12 reps per leg - Lower body

Progressive Overload at Home

Increase reps, decrease rest time, or add resistance bands to keep progressing without weights.
