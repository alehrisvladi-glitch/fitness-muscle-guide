Effective Home Workouts

Get fit at home with these bodyweight exercises. No gym equipment needed!

Essential Bodyweight Exercises

  • Push-ups: 3-4 sets x 10-20 reps - Chest, shoulders, triceps
  • Pull-ups: 3-4 sets x 5-10 reps - Back and biceps
  • Squats: 3-4 sets x 15-25 reps - Legs and glutes
  • Planks: 3 sets x 30-60 seconds - Core strength

Progression Tips

Increase difficulty by adding reps, slowing tempo, or using variations like decline push-ups and pistol squats.
