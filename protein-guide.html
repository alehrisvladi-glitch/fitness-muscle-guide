Complete Protein & Nutrition Guide

Master your nutrition for maximum muscle growth and recovery.

Protein Recommendations

  • Daily Intake: 1.6-2.2g per kg of bodyweight for muscle building
  • Timing: Spread protein intake across 4-5 meals throughout the day
  • Post-Workout: 20-40g of whey protein within 2 hours after training
  • Whole Foods: Chicken, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt, lean beef

Calorie Surplus

To build muscle, eat 250-500 calories above maintenance. Track your intake and adjust based on progress.
