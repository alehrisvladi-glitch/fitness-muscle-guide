Science-Backed Supplements

Evidence-based supplement recommendations for muscle building and performance.

Top Supplements

  • Creatine Monohydrate: 5g daily - Increases strength and muscle mass
  • Whey Protein: 20-40g post-workout - Convenient protein source
  • Caffeine: 200-400mg pre-workout - Boosts energy and focus
  • Vitamin D3: 2000-4000 IU daily - Overall health and testosterone

What to Avoid

Most pre-workout blends, BCAAs (if eating enough protein), and fat burners have minimal evidence. Focus on basics first.
